Bundled CSL assets originate from the Citation Style Language project.

- GB/T 7714—2015 numeric style: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles/blob/master/china-national-standard-gb-t-7714-2015-numeric.csl
- Chinese locale: https://github.com/citation-style-language/locales/blob/master/locales-zh-CN.xml
- Style licensing: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), as declared in the original file. Original author and contributor information is retained in the XML.
- Locale licensing: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported, per the upstream locales repository.

Retrieval date and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in sources.json. The application labels the style by its explicit 2015 edition; it does not assert that edition is the latest standard.
